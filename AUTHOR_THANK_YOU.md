# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Xi Huang, Lixing Zhang, and Di Luo for developing D-RUT, connecting quantum measurement design with structured polynomial recovery, and making the protocol's Heisenberg-limit, SPAM, hierarchical-recovery, and first-quantized goals explicit enough to audit.

This repository is an independent clean-room classical audit. It keeps the paper authors and their claims clearly attributed, records which finite proxies were checked, and leaves the missing quantum protocol, strict C4 comparison, and C6 IDFT route explicit.
